About

I'm Liliana

I taught myself programming and Unity, and later completed an Associate's Degree in Software Development. I got into the game industry in October 2023, driven by how much I genuinely enjoy making games.

Since then, I've been working on SOPA — Tale of the Stolen Potato as a Gameplay Developer and Lighting Artist. The game launched in 2025 on Xbox and Steam, and I'm now continuing work on its sequel.

These days I use AI tools, including agent-based workflows, to speed up my process, prototype ideas faster, and keep improving how I work.

I also come from a Psychology background, which really helps me communicate and collaborate with teams. I'm especially interested in games with an educational purpose and those with rich narratives and outstanding artwork.
